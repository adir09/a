## 📧 Cold Outreach Email

**Body:**

>

>
> I’m a technical consultant who works with solo founders, and I noticed a few things on your site that could potentially cause scaling issues as you grow (specifically [mention a minor technical detail like page speed or a specific API use]).

> [Your Name]

## 📈 Go-To-Market Strategy

1. **Daily Presence:** Spend 15 minutes a day on Twitter/X searching for "solo founder tech stack" or "how to build SaaS" and provide genuine value in the replies. Link your site only when relevant.
2. **Directory Listings:** List your service on Indie Hackers, Betalist, and specialized consulting directories.
3. **Free Value:** Share one "Technical Tip of the Day" on LinkedIn to build authority.
